About Us

Paresseuse is a 2-piece band from Toronto, formed in 2026 by Jimmy Todorov (guitar, bass, vocals) and Joe Morgenstern (drums, keys). Joe’s jazz vocabulary pairs well with Jimmy’s use of alternate tunings and his penchant for heavier sounds. The pair have been writing songs together since they were 10 years old.

Their debut album Effacer was released on November 4, 2026, led by the singles “Teeth” and “Montreal”.

For fans of: Alice in Chains, Steely Dan, Soundgarden, Joni Mitchell, and Midlake

EFFACER

"On Effacer, Paresseuse bring jazz-pop harmony and grunge’s heavier guitar textures into close conversation. The Toronto duo of Jimmy Todorov and Joe Morgenstern build their 12-song full-length record from layered acoustic and electric guitars, lush keys and a rhythm section shaped by jazz fusion. Soft, slow passages sit alongside harder, heavier ones, with pop melody providing a thread through the changes. It is music rooted in the meeting of two distinct musical instincts." (via Songplode)
